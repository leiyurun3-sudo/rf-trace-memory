The operator-restart and RF/KV-development series reuse R01-R06 identifiers and are indexed separately. A delivery/update bundle describes runnable inputs; a results bundle contains historical outputs.

**Coverage gaps:** standalone R60, R63 and R64 archives were not located in the current inventory.

**Separate holdings:** synthetic smoke fixtures, mixed-project bundles, unreviewed nested archives and handoff screenshot bundles are omitted. They require correct labeling, project separation or content review before standalone release.
