SOURCE-CENTERED LOSO: EXACT DEFINITIONS AND HELD-OUT Y SCOPE
Let y_sa be the seed-averaged outcome for source s and arm a in population A_s; f_sa is the selected feature vector. Define ybar_s=mean_{a in A_s}(y_sa), fbar_s=mean_{a in A_s}(f_sa), ytilde_sa=y_sa-ybar_s, and ftilde_sa=f_sa-fbar_s. Transform I before centering: centered I^2 is I_sa^2-mean_a(I_sa^2), not (I_sa-mean_a I_sa)^2. Indicators are centered in the same way. Fit beta_{-s} to all centered rows from other sources without intercept, then predict ftilde_sa^T beta_{-s}. The held-out source's full observed outcome profile supplies ybar_s solely for defining the centered scoring targets. It does not enter beta, predictor centering, model selection, or an absolute prediction reconstruction. Because held-out Y is required to define the target, this is a conditional profile/contrast evaluation, not label-free prospective prediction of absolute outcomes. Centering each source before LOSO is algebraically source-local; all seeds of s are excluded from fitting together.

Arm populations (complete source-seed support required separately for each outcome): R84 local_dose=CORR15/CORR25/CORR35 (30 source-arm rows, 10 sources; regional 24 rows, 8 sources); R84 same_memory adds ENERGY (40/32 rows); R84 with_memory further adds BOTH (50/40 rows). R85 same_memory=CORR25/ENERGY/U25 (120 rows, 40 sources; regional 99 rows, 33 sources); R85 with_memory adds BOTH (160/132 rows). The same_memory indicator is local residual allocation versus global residual direction; with_memory adds a separate BOTH memory-release flag. No external algorithm is assigned zero comparable dose.

CV skill=1-sum_{s,a}(ytilde_sa-yhat_sa)^2/sum_{s,a}ytilde_sa^2. Negative skill means worse squared error than predicting zero source-centered deviation. RMSE is pooled over source-arm rows; complete arm support gives equal weight to each source. Raw CV baseline is the training-source outcome mean, not the held-out mean. For transfer, raw baseline is the R84 training mean; centered baseline remains zero.

R84->R85 TRANSFER DESIGN: R84 same_memory (CORR15/CORR25/CORR35/ENERGY) fits transferred unchanged to R85 common policies CORR25/ENERGY. Training centers use the four R84 arms; held-out evaluation centers use the two R85 arms. Thus the intervention menu and population differ; centered R85 evaluation concerns a two-policy contrast. No R85 coefficient fit, no U25 unseen-policy extrapolation, and no BOTH extrapolation. R84 full metrics: 10 sources/40 training rows; regional: 8/32. R85 full: 40 sources/80 evaluation rows; regional: 33/66. Four evaluation rows lie outside the R84 training impulse range in either support.

PAPER-READY SHORT ENGLISH METHODS PARAGRAPH
We first averaged the complete planned seeds within each source and configuration. For each outcome and comparison population, we centered the outcome and each transformed predictor (I, I-squared, or log I, plus the included indicators) over that source's eligible configurations. Least-squares models were fit without an intercept, leaving out all configurations and seeds of one source at a time. For a held-out source, its observed outcome mean was used only to define the centered evaluation targets; no held-out outcomes entered coefficient estimation. Thus this cross-validation assesses conditional within-source configuration profiles, rather than absolute prediction for an unseen source with unavailable outcome labels. Prediction skill was defined as 1 minus pooled held-out squared error divided by the squared error of the zero-deviation baseline. Raw models used an intercept and the training-set outcome mean as baseline. All candidate forms were exploratory.
